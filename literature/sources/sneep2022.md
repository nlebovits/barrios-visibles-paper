The SNEEP 2022 report gives an official year-end prison population of
105,053. The census implies roughly 107,000 incarcerated residents.

This is one of the few places where an independent administrative figure
can be held against the census, and the census comes out close. It
argues against a large prison undercount and leaves the rest of the
collective-dwelling population untested.
