Moultrie and Dorrington publish a peer-reviewed critique of the South
African Census 2022 post-enumeration survey. The census-coverage working
notes require this source to accompany any use of the 31 percent
undercount figure.

It is recorded in the registry as a contradiction against the
comparative-range claim, so that the disputed South African number
cannot be pulled in as support without the critique appearing next to
it.
