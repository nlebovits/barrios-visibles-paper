Fundación Tejido Urbano measured the published population of CABA's
villas between 2010 and 2022 and found that Villa 1-11-14 fell from
28,259 to 6,868, an apparent loss of about 76 percent. Checking radio by
radio, the decline appeared throughout the settlement, and the analysts
judged the 2022 values unreliable enough to exclude.

The value here is independence. Tejido Urbano found the anomaly in final
tract data, and IDECBA later traced a closely related shortfall through
operational field sheets. Different inputs, same barrio, same
conclusion.
