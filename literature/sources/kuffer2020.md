Kuffer and colleagues set out the role of Earth observation in an
integrated deprived-area mapping system for low and middle income
countries. The paper describes the circularity that makes informal
settlements hard to count.

Where no deprived-area map exists, census and household-survey
sampling underreport those communities, and the underreporting keeps
the map from being made. Small, temporary, unrecognised, and most
deprived areas are the likeliest omissions.
