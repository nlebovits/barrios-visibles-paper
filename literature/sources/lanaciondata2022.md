La Nación Data collected, verified, and mapped reader reports of
households that enumerators never visited, immediately after Census Day
2022.

It establishes that real operational omissions happened and were visible
at the time. It shows no geographic concentration, so it cannot support
an argument that informal settlements bore the omissions.
