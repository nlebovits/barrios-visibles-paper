Leete's symposium paper reviews the funding position of national statistical
offices after the 2000 census round. The title poses a funding crisis as the
question. The manuscript cites it for the institutional reason why
census data drifts out of step with informal settlement populations.

It is background rather than evidence. The paper is from 2001 and concerns
resource-constrained statistical systems, so its weight in the Argentine
argument is small.
