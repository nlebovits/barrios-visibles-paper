Carr-Hill argues that household-survey frames, and in some settings
censuses, systematically exclude mobile, homeless, institutionalised,
and conflict-affected groups, and estimates roughly 250 million people
missing from common development-measurement systems.

The manuscript cites this source as corroborating context. It is weak
evidence for an Argentine effect size and strong evidence that the
exclusion problem is structural and long documented.
