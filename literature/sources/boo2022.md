Boo and colleagues model population at high resolution across five provinces
of the Democratic Republic of the Congo, calibrating satellite-derived
footprint area against complete household enumeration in 926 microcensus
clusters. Out-of-sample performance on population totals reaches an R-squared
of 0.79, with about 90 percent of observations inside nominal 95 percent
credible intervals.

This is the methodological warrant for the central move in Barrios Visibles.
It also settles the direction of footprint error. Missing, outdated, and
obscured footprints inflate modelled densities and push the population
estimate down, so detection failure makes a footprint-derived estimate
conservative.

It does not supply a dwelling yield for Argentina. The calibration is local to
the DRC microcensus and includes non-residential buildings.
