Brazil's 2022 census reports universe results for favelas and urban
communities, covering 6.56 million dwellings. It is the closest large Latin
American benchmark available for the occupancy of informal-settlement housing.

Use it to bound the dwelling yield, not to validate coverage. The census
measured the dwellings it found. Whether it found all of them is the open
question, and this publication cannot answer it.
