Published census totals can include large numbers of imputed residents.
Imputation operates only on dwellings or households the census system has
already identified, which leaves out hidden subdivisions, rear dwellings,
upper-floor households, and buildings missing from the census frame.

A published total counts records rather than people reached.
Brazil's 2022 census directly enumerated about 195.1 million people and published 203.1
million after imputing for occupied households without completed interviews.
