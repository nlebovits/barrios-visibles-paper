INDEC's press release on the definitive 2022 results carries the figures
for the populations most exposed to undercount: 5,705 people in street
situations and 267,793 residents of collective dwellings, implying about
107,000 incarcerated people.

The arithmetic is the point. Even a several-fold undercount of the
street population moves a 45.9 million national total by hundredths of a
percentage point. High relative risk and low national leverage are
compatible, and confusing them weakens the argument.
