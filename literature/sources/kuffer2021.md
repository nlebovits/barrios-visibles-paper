Kuffer and colleagues document spatial information gaps on deprived
urban areas through surveys and workshops with governments, community
organisations, NGOs, and researchers.

The value here is that the gap is reported by the people who work with
the data. Base maps are outdated, partial, and sparsest exactly where
settlements grow fastest, and practitioners name building data as the
input they want for population models.
