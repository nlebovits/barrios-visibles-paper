Almeida and colleagues estimate population in Rio das Pedras, a
multistory favela, using object-based image analysis and 3D modelling.
A height factor equivalent to a 30 percent increase over orthogonally
projected residential area produced 43,295 residents against a
census-projected 43,342.

The close agreement is suggestive rather than conclusive, and the
venue is not peer-reviewed. The value for this project is the
direction and rough size of the vertical correction in a Latin
American setting.
