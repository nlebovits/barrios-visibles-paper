---
id: reconciliation-does-not-repair-small-area-geography
scope: international
requires_direct_measurement: false
---

A national demographic reconciliation leaves missing and misallocated people
in small-area census geography exactly where they were. A revised national
estimate changes the demographic baseline, and the published small-area tables
stay as published.

Brazil is the clearest case. The 2024 projection revision incorporated the
2022 census, the post-enumeration survey, and demographic reconciliation for
all 27 states, producing 210.9 million for July 1, 2022. The published census
tables were left untouched, so the original small-area geography retains its
original errors.
