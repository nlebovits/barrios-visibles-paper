INDEC's provisional results for the 2022 census, published 31 January
2023, gave 46,044,703 people: 45,767,858 in private dwellings, 273,883
in collective dwellings, and 2,962 in street situations. The definitive
total later came in at 45,892,285.

The interest is the gap between the two. Records moved during
processing, and that processing window is where the documented Padre
Ricciardelli loss occurred.
