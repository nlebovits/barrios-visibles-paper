Kit, Lüdeke, and Reckien assess slum population in Hyderabad with
satellite imagery and field visits. The results cut both ways, which
is why the registry retains the source as counterevidence.

Image-derived estimates exceeded official data in one city circle and
fell below the official share citywide. Some officially listed slums
no longer looked morphologically slum-like. Imagery is not uniformly
biased upward against official records.
