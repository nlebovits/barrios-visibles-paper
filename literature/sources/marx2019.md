Marx, Stoker, and Suri study ethnic patronage in Kibera, combining
household and GPS survey data with very high resolution image
segmentation. The segmentation produced objects the authors describe
as roofs or blocks of roofs, manually corrected.

The 11.2 households per roof object figure is the registry's strongest
evidence that one delineated polygon can conceal many households. Read
the object definition carefully before transferring the number. A roof
block is coarser than a building, so 11.2 is not a
households-per-building ratio.
