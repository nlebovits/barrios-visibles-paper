The Subsecretaría de Integración Socio Urbana manual sets out how RENABAP
builds and updates the registry of barrios populares. Page 24 is the
load-bearing passage for this project. The approximate count of occupied
dwellings in each barrio combines four inputs: the community referentes, the
fieldworker's own tally of dwellings and blocks, a dwelling count read from
satellite imagery during quality control, and later house-to-house surveys.

The manual matters twice. It is the institutional warrant for counting
dwellings from imagery, because RENABAP already does it. It is also the
documented half of the independence problem. A registry built on community
referentes and social organizations uses the same field infrastructure as any
census operation that reaches households the same way.
