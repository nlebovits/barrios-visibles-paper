Samper and colleagues analyse the spatiotemporal dynamics of informal
settlements across Argentine cities at national scale, using RENABAP
polygons. Juan Manuel D'Attoli, the RENABAP coordinator, is a
coauthor.

Informal settlement area is concentrated outside the large metropolitan
areas, and it is growing faster there. Both results cut against treating
the Buenos Aires metropolitan region as representative of the national
picture.
