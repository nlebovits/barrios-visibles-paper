---
id: territorial-coverage-is-not-population-coverage
scope: argentina
requires_direct_measurement: false
---

A reported territorial coverage rate is the share of predefined census
segments completed, not the share of residents counted. Segment populations
vary, so the two quantities come apart wherever population density does.

Argentina's 2022 census reported 98.6 percent territorial coverage. San Juan
reached 93.9 percent, the Ciudad Autónoma de Buenos Aires 97.4 percent, and
Formosa and Mendoza 96.8 percent. Each figure counts segments, and the census
reports population coverage nowhere in that table.
