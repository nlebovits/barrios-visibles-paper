Sacco's peer-reviewed analysis tests whether Argentina's census numbers
are demographically coherent. For 2010 he obtains about 2.1 percent
omission, with a range of 1.4 to 2.1 percent depending on migration
assumptions.

His overall judgement is the most balanced in the literature. The count
is broadly coherent and omission is low by regional standards, while the
absence of direct evaluation, the unpublished procedures, and the
delayed documentation all weaken credibility.

One caution matters for this project. His sensitivity exercise reaches
about 6 percent under broad assumptions about occupied-but-absent
dwellings, which is the magnitude Barrios Visibles reports. He presents
it as a speculative boundary. Citing it as his estimate would
misrepresent him.
