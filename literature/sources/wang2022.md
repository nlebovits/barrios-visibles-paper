Wang and colleagues assess what can be learned about urban morphology
from space, and find that incomplete or suboptimal open building maps
still contain substantial information.

The pair of findings is the useful part. Imperfect footprint data is
usable, and its errors propagate, so the right response is sensitivity
analysis rather than either rejection or uncritical use.
