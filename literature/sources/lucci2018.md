Lucci, Bhatkal, and Khan ask whether standard measurement understates urban
poverty, and find that it does. The manuscript cites the paper as corroborating
context alongside breuer2024, carrhill2013, and kuffer2022.

Its value is in the consequence rather than the measurement. If informal
settlement residents are missing from the frame, they are missing from the
poverty statistics that drive allocation too.
