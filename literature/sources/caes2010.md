A five-university academic oversight council reviewed INDEC before the
2010 census and reported limited external consultation, a narrowly
located pilot, weak documentation of the experimental census, and no
apparent plan for a post-census coverage survey.

The timing is what gives this weight. The missing post-enumeration
survey was predicted, in writing, by an external body before the count
happened. It is strong evidence about quality-assurance design and no
evidence at all that the resulting total was wrong.
