Peru's INEI enumerated 29,381,884 people in the 2017 census, estimated
1,855,501 omitted through the post-enumeration Encuesta de Evaluación
Censal, and published an adjusted total of 31,237,385. The omission rate
is 5.94 percent.

This is the most useful comparative case in the registry because the
proportion is nearly identical to the Argentine discrepancy and the
method measures coverage directly.
