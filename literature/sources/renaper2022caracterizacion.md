RENAPER's characterisation of international migration reports 3,033,786
foreign-born people holding a current digital identity or residence in
Argentina in August 2022, after attempting to remove those abroad for
more than six months. The census counted 1,933,463 foreign-born people
in private dwellings.

The 1.10 million difference is the largest administrative-versus-census
gap in the Argentine record. It is also the one where definitions
diverge most, because a valid document is not residence on census night.
The gap is a warning signal rather than a measurement.
