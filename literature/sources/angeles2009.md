Angeles and colleagues design and run the 2005 census and mapping of
slums in Bangladesh, combining satellite imagery with field
enumeration across six cities. The combined approach identified 9,048
slum communities.

The registry retains this source partly as counterevidence. Imagery
alone missed roughly 30 percent of slums because of foliage, terrain,
morphological variation, and recent development. That omission runs in
the same direction as the census omission, not against it.
