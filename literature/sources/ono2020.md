Ono and Kidokoro document development patterns of informal settlements
in Nairobi through fieldwork in three villages of Mukuru Kwa Njenga.
They record building heights, plot coverage, floor-area ratio, and
tenure arrangements.

The coverage-to-floor-area comparison is the clearest statement of why
a planar count understates housing. Footprint covers 64.7 percent of
the ground while floor area reaches 196.7 percent of it. The
rental-room rows then show that the extra floor area is subdivided
into separate households.
