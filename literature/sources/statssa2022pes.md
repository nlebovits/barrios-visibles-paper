Statistics South Africa's post-enumeration survey for the 2022 census
reported a net person undercount of 31.06 percent and a household
undercount of 30.49 percent.

The registry retains it as a bounded caution. The figure is an extreme
modern outlier, its methodology is contested, and it belongs in any
discussion only next to the peer-reviewed critique.
