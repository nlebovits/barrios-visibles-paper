Ocar, Nadali, and Labat review Argentina's last three censuses through
the experience of Misiones province.

No national post-enumeration survey accompanied the 2010 or 2022 rounds,
so every coverage figure for those years is a model-based
reconciliation. The Misiones account also documents late method changes
and recovery counting during the operation, which describes the
conditions under which records go missing.

literature review supplied the correct names.
