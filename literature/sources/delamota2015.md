De la Mota's AEPA paper evaluates coverage and quality of the 2010
census and, in passing, records that a 2001 post-enumeration survey was
conducted and never published. An INDEC demographer reported that it
produced omission values far above both the operational and the
demographic estimates.

The 2001 figure keeps an asterisk throughout the project for this reason.
The measurement that would settle it exists and is unavailable.
