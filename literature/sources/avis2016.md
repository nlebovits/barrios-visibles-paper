Avis reviews urban governance challenges around informal settlements for
GSDRC. The manuscript cites the topic guide for government non-recognition as
a cause of missing data.

Argentina is a partial counterexample worth recording. RENABAP names the
settlements, so non-recognition is not the mechanism at work there. That makes
the Argentine result harder to explain away, not easier.
