### S5.4 Adjacent-radio diagnostic

The recovered validation pass classified radios with at least 1% barrio coverage, radios within 2 km but below that coverage, and more distant radios. The table reports urban radios only.

| Radio class | Radios | Population | Median population/km² | Persons per private dwelling |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Contains barrio | 7,695 | 7,795,633 | 8,720 | 3.18 |
| Border, below 1% coverage | 30,285 | 24,342,739 | 11,394 | 2.55 |
| Distant (>2 km) | 20,391 | 12,110,393 | 3,028 | 2.33 |

The saved output shows no elevated persons-per-private-dwelling ratio in adjacent radios. Raw density is higher because adjacent radios cluster in dense urban areas. The result cannot independently confirm that the national Census total is low. The validation output was preserved, but the exact distance-query implementation was not, so this table is not yet fully reproducible from the repository.
