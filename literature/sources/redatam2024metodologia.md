INDEC's methodological notes for the 2022 Census REDATAM database set out the
formal concepts, the geographic units, the household and dwelling variables,
the data products, and the dissemination structure. This is the reference for
interpreting the radio-level household and population figures the analysis
compares footprints against.

It is recorded here for what it does not contain. The document describes what
INDEC measured and cannot speak to how enumerators found households in
informal settlements. Treating formal methodology as evidence of operational
independence would be the error this registry is built to catch.
