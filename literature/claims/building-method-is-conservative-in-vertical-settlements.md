id: building-method-is-conservative-in-vertical-settlements
scope: method
requires_direct_measurement: false
---

The Barrios Visibles method is conservative where settlements are dense and
vertically developed. Where INDEC reports a higher population than the
building-based floor, the analysis retains INDEC's value, and it never infers
extra households inside one footprint.

Hidden vertical and subdivided households push the estimate downward.
Enumerators in Villa 21-24 described buildings up to five stories, several
households behind one apparent entrance, and an inquilinato holding as many as
ten households. The footprint-based floor excludes all of them.
