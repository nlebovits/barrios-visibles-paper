---
id: census-net-omission-small-but-nonzero
scope: argentina
---

Argentina's published national census totals are credible within low
single-digit margins. The best-supported net omission is 2.75 percent for
2001, roughly 1 to 2.5 percent for 2010, and 0.5 percent for 2022. None of
these figures is zero.
