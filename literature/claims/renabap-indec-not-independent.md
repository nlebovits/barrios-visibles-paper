id: renabap-indec-not-independent
scope: argentina
---

RENABAP and the 2022 Census are institutionally distinct, and in the Ciudad
Autónoma de Buenos Aires their measurements were not fully independent. Both
relied on barrio-level community knowledge and organisational infrastructure
to identify, estimate, and reach households.

Agreement between RENABAP and INDEC is weak evidence of accuracy. Omissions
produced by shared field infrastructure appear in both datasets.

Both systems produce low barrio-level denominators against the visible
housing stock. Footprints per RENABAP family run about 1.59, and
footprints per INDEC household in the cleanest nearly-contained radios run
about 1.55. That convergence is consistent with correlated coverage failure
rather than with mutual confirmation.

The operational overlap is documented for CABA only. Extending it to other
provinces needs provincial documentation that nobody has located.
