# RENABAP and INDEC: shared enumeration mechanisms

## Bottom line

RENABAP and the 2022 Census are institutionally distinct and do not publish the same type of statistic. RENABAP reports approximate families and occupied dwellings associated with registered *barrios populares*. INDEC reports people, households, and dwellings enumerated through the national census.

However, the two measurements are not fully independent. The available evidence shows that both relied on barrio-level intermediaries and social organizations to identify and reach households. RENABAP formally incorporates estimates from community *referentes* and surveys organized through social organizations. Contemporary reporting about the 2022 Census shows the same kind of infrastructure being used in CABA's villas.

This overlap means that agreement between RENABAP and INDEC cannot be treated as strong independent validation. Shared upstream mechanisms can produce correlated omissions.

The operational overlap is directly documented for CABA. Extending that conclusion to every province would require additional provincial documentation.

## 1. How RENABAP estimates dwellings and families

### Primary source

**Argentina, Secretaría de Integración Socio Urbana. _Manual para la conformación y actualización del Registro Nacional de Barrios Populares_.** See p. 24 and Annex IV.

- PDF: [Manual para la conformación y actualización del RENABAP](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/sites/default/files/manual-para-la-conformacion-y-actualizacion-del-renabap.pdf)
- Program page: [Registro Nacional de Barrios Populares (RENABAP)](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/habitat/renabap)

### What the manual directly establishes

The manual says that the approximate number of occupied dwellings in each barrio is adjusted using three complementary sources:

1. The approximate number reported by the barrio's community *referente* or *referentes*.
2. The number perceived by the fieldworker after counting dwellings and blocks in the territory.
3. A dwelling count made from satellite imagery during quality control.

The operative phrases are “referenta/es comunitarios,” “conteo de viviendas y manzanas en territorio,” and “imágenes satelitales.”

The manual then describes a later updating stage in which the RENABAP coordination, working through the social organizations already mentioned, conducts house-to-house family surveys in every barrio. These visits continue adjusting the estimated number of occupied dwellings, barrio boundaries, and the urban diagnosis.

The manual describes the later family survey as using the “modalidad casa por casa” through the social organizations previously identified.

Annex IV explains how fieldworkers identify barrio *referentes*. Together, these passages directly establish that RENABAP's dwelling and family estimates depend partly on community knowledge, territorial fieldwork, social organizations, and satellite-image review.

## 2. How the 2022 Census operated in CABA's villas

### Contemporary source

**Clarín. “Censo 2022: cómo se relevará a la población en las villas porteñas.” 6 May 2022.**

The article was also indexed by Universidad Torcuato Di Tella's “Di Tella en los medios” archive. The title and publication date are secure; the author field should be verified before importing the item into Zotero.

The current Clarín result redirects to the publisher's homepage rather than preserving a stable article URL: [Clarín](https://www.clarin.com/). Locate the archived article by its exact title before treating the citation record as complete.

### What the article directly establishes

The contemporary report says that:

- Census enumeration in the city's villas was assigned to the **Ministerio de Desarrollo Humano y Hábitat**.
- The operation was being coordinated through barrio *referentes*, delegates, recognized residents, and social organizations.
- Preparatory population projections began with the 2010 Census and were updated using subsequent local information.

The key statement was:

> Estamos coordinando con los referentes barriales, con los delegados y los vecinos más reconocidos; también con las organizaciones sociales.

This is direct evidence that the CABA Census operation relied on the same categories of territorial intermediaries that RENABAP formally uses.

## 3. Supporting INDEC documentation

### Sources

- INDEC, [Censo Nacional de Población, Hogares y Viviendas 2022](https://www.indec.gob.ar/indec/web/Nivel4-Tema-2-18-77).
- INDEC, [Aspectos metodológicos de la base REDATAM del Censo 2022](https://redatam.indec.gob.ar/redarg/CENSOS/CPV2022/Docs/Redatam_aspectos_metodologicos.pdf).

### What these sources establish

These sources document the Census's formal concepts, geographic units, household and dwelling variables, data products, and dissemination structure. They are useful for establishing what INDEC measured and how the published radio-level data should be interpreted.

They do **not** independently establish the operational reliance on barrio organizations. That evidence comes from the contemporary account of the CABA enumeration.

## 4. What follows from comparing the sources

The following conclusion is a synthesis, not a quotation from either institution:

> RENABAP and the 2022 Census were institutionally distinct, but their measurements in CABA's informal settlements were not fully independent. Both relied on barrio-level community knowledge and organizational infrastructure to identify, estimate, and reach households.

The methodological consequence is:

> Agreement between RENABAP and INDEC is weak evidence of accuracy because omissions created by shared field infrastructure could appear in both datasets.

The sources do **not** show that:

- INDEC copied RENABAP's published family totals.
- RENABAP and INDEC used the same questionnaire or database.
- Every province delegated Census operations in barrios populares through the same arrangement documented in CABA.
- Operational overlap alone proves a national population undercount of approximately three million people.

## 5. Relationship to the Barrios Visibles results

The empirical comparison uses a third measurement source: building footprints spatially joined to RENABAP polygons and compared with INDEC's 2022 radio-level household and population data.

- Project: [Barrios Visibles](https://barriosvisibles.org/)
- Working paper: [Lebovits, _Barrios Visibles_](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=6588819)

Relevant results from the analysis:

| Comparison | Result |
|---|---:|
| Building footprints per RENABAP family | approximately 1.59 |
| Building footprints per INDEC household in the cleanest nearly-contained radios | approximately 1.55 |
| RENABAP families | 1,237,795 |
| INDEC households allocated inside RENABAP polygons by dasymetric mapping | approximately 997,000 |
| INDEC households extrapolated from radios at least 95% contained within RENABAP polygons | approximately 1.27 million |

RENABAP and INDEC therefore produce similarly low barrio-level denominators relative to the visible housing stock. Because the institutional evidence shows overlap in how households were located and reached, this convergence is consistent with correlated coverage failure. It is not independent confirmation that both counts are complete.
