INDEC's publication on Indigenous and descendant population reports a
rise from 955,032 in 2010 to 1,306,730 in 2022. The 2022 questionnaire
asked the question universally at person level, while 2010 used an
expanded sample with household-level questioning.

The registry keeps this as a discipline case. Most of the increase is
the instrument changing, not the population. It is about how enumerated
people were classified, which is a different question from whether
people were enumerated.
