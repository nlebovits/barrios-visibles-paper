Gunter maps squatter settlements in Johannesburg using Google Earth
and ILWIS. The registry records the paper because kuffer2016 reports
that its image mapping found about 10,000 more dwellings in one
settlement than government estimates recorded.

The finding has not been verified in the original. It is the
closest reported dwelling-count discrepancy to the Barrios Visibles
result, which makes retrieving the paper a priority rather than an
optional follow-up.
