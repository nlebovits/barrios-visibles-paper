A national census omission near 6 percent lies inside the range modern Latin
American censuses have reported. A discrepancy of roughly three million people
against Argentina's 2022 population is surprising and not inherently
implausible.

Cases must be labelled by method, because a directly measured omission and a
demographic adjustment are different quantities. Peru measured 5.94 percent
through a post-enumeration survey. Brazil measured 12.2 percent person
omission and 8.3 percent net coverage error the same way. Colombia and
Guatemala report figures near 8.5 and 9 percent through demographic
reconciliation instead.
